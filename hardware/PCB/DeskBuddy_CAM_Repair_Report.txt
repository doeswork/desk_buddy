DESKBUDDY SMD v1.0 - CAM GEOMETRY REPAIR - 2026-09-19

This archive contains revised manufacturing files, not an EasyEDA source project.

Changes:
- Board outline replaced by one closed six-segment contour following the
  original main border. Overlapping/retraced left-edge spurs removed.
- Only narrow 0.254 mm signal routes edited to improve copper spacing and
  bottom-layer edge margin.
- The redundant Drill_PTH_Through_Via.DRL was removed. Every one of its ten
  holes is already present in Drill_PTH_Through.DRL.

Use Drill_PTH_Through.DRL for ALL plated holes and slots.
Use Drill_NPTH_Through.DRL for the non-plated mounting hole.
Do not add the removed via-only drill file back into this package.

Main outline dimensions: 40.132 x 80.010 mm, with the original corner step.
The coordinate origin and all component/pad/hole positions are unchanged.
Solder mask, paste, silkscreen and the main drill files are unchanged.

Checks:
- Pad/via-flash connectivity grouping unchanged (196 flashes, 45 groups).
- Unique hole/slot set unchanged: 80 round plated holes, 4 plated slots,
  and 1 non-plated hole.
- Approx. minimum spacing between separate copper regions:
  top 0.212 mm; bottom 0.206 mm.
- Minimum copper-to-outline centerline: top 0.751 mm; bottom 0.418 mm.

LIMITS:
This is a Gerber geometry repair, NOT schematic-aware electrical DRC approval.
No netlist, schematic, BOM or native PCB source was present in the input ZIP.
Existing electrical intent, connector pinouts, component ratings, power-rail
capacity and antenna clearance have NOT been certified. No new ground plane
was added, and the original wide power routing was not resized or rewired.
Existing shorts or missing connections, if any, are not ruled out by a
before/after connectivity match.

Review this archive in your fabricator's viewer and complete a schematic-aware
review before authorizing manufacture. The original EasyEDA project has not
been updated; exporting it again will not include these CAM-only repairs.

Outline detail:
The original contour included a left-edge protrusion to Y=173.736 mm and
retraced segments above the main horizontal edge at Y=173.482 mm. The start
and end of the exported path differed by 0.127 mm, but the issue was more
specifically overlapping/dangling contour segments, not simply a clean gap.
The revised profile follows the original six main corners, in absolute mm:
  (20.955, 93.472)
  (61.087, 93.472)
  (61.087,155.575)
  (56.007,155.575)
  (56.007,173.482)
  (20.955,173.482)
  back to (20.955,93.472)
The redundant 0.254 mm protruding stub is not retained. The original
0.320 mm outline plotting aperture is retained; dimensions and edge distances
are measured to the profile CENTERLINE, not the outside of that stroke.

Copper edits:
All edited traces retain their original 0.254 mm width. End connections,
pad apertures, via flashes and hole centers are preserved.
- Bottom: moved the long left-edge trace centerline from X=21.37384 mm
  to X=21.50000 mm and adjusted its diagonal entries.
- Bottom: shifted the Y=143.09115 mm signal run to Y=143.02000 mm and
  adjusted its diagonal entries, clearing the nearby pad and via.
- Bottom: shifted the inner right-header route from X=46.95215 mm to
  X=46.88000 mm; adjusted the Y~120 mm bend and long diagonal for clearance.
- Top: adjusted the two diagonals around the right-hand header near
  Y=100-102 mm without moving any pad.
- Top: shifted the inner right-header run from X=46.92167 mm to
  X=46.88000 mm with matching diagonal entries.
- Top: shifted the long X=50.25720 mm signal run to X=50.30000 mm,
  then returned it to the original via center at (50.25720,143.70507) mm.
No 1.0, 1.8 or 0.3 mm trace geometry was changed. No ground fill was added.

Validation method:
The actual RS-274X files were parsed locally; copper flashes and line sweeps
were reconstructed as geometric shapes. Circular edges were approximated by
256-sided polygons (geometry error below about 0.0001 mm at these radii).
All polygons were combined per layer; distances between physically separate
copper islands and to the intended outline were measured. Numeric results
are best interpreted rounded to 0.001 mm, not as manufacturing tolerances.
A graph joined touching copper primitives and plated holes across layers.
The BEFORE and AFTER partitions of all 196 pad/via flashes are identical.
All 85 distinct drilled hole/slot geometries match, and remain inside the
board. The replacement outline is a single closed, simple six-edge ring.
Unchanged files were compared byte-for-byte. Final ZIP CRCs were tested.
Top/bottom previews were rendered from the repaired files and inspected.
This was custom geometric validation, not EasyEDA/KiCad/JLCPCB electrical DRC
or an independent manufacturer's CAM acceptance test.

Why 0.20 mm was used as a clearance target:
The original approximately 0.154 mm gaps are not automatically defects for
1 oz production. JLCPCB's currently published capabilities specify 0.10 mm
track/space for 1 oz, 0.16 mm for 2 oz on two-layer boards, and 0.20 mm
copper clearance from routed board edges. The repair uses more margin where
possible; the board's final copper weight/thickness and current capacity
still need separate confirmation. These figures are NOT a blanket
manufacturability approval for every aspect of the board.
Reference checked 2026-09-19:
https://jlcpcb.com/capabilities/pcb-capabilities
